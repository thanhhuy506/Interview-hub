How to answer like a senior candidate

1. Start directlyGive the definition in one or two sentences. Do not begin with a long story.
2. Explain the purposeShow why the practice exists and which problem it solves.
3. Add a real exampleUse a short example from delivery, quality, dependencies, risk, or team coaching.
4. Mention trade-offsA senior answer explains misuse, limitations, and context—not only the happy path.
5. Use Scrum language carefullySay accountabilities, Developers, Sprint Goal, Product Goal, and Definition of Done.
6. Focus on outcomesConnect Scrum practices to customer value, feedback speed, risk reduction, and team effectiveness.
