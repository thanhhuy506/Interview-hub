The speech

My First Month at Rakuten

About 2 minutes

Good evening, everyone!

First of all, thank you for organizing this welcome party and giving me the opportunity to share a few words.

It's been almost a month since I joined Rakuten Fintech Vietnam, and I can say that it has been a great learning experience for me.

During this time, I've learned a lot, especially about Rakuten's company culture, Rakuten Shugi, and the Five Principles for Success. These principles have not only helped me understand the company's values, but also inspired me to improve myself. I believe they are useful not only at work, but also in our everyday lives.

I've also received valuable training about our projects and the credit card business. In particular, I've started learning about RADECO, the debt collection project I'm joining. There is still a lot for me to learn, but I'm really excited about it.

One thing that has impressed me the most is the working environment. Everyone is professional, positive, and always willing to help. I truly appreciate the support and kindness I've received from my colleagues.

Although I'm still new and haven't had the chance to get to know everyone yet, I hope to become more familiar with the project, build good relationships with my colleagues, and contribute to the success of Rakuten Fintech Vietnam.

Once again, thank you so much for the warm welcome. I look forward to learning from all of you, working together, and growing as part of the team.

Thank you, everyone!

Before you speak

Small tips, big impact.

  • Smile and look around the room after your greeting.
  • Slow down when you mention Rakuten Shugi and the Five Principles.
  • Pause briefly before talking about the supportive team.
  • Finish warmly. Don't rush the last “Thank you.”

01 / The big picture

What is Rakuten Shugi?

English: Rakuten Shugi means “Rakuten Basic Principles.” It is the shared set of values and ways of working that guides employees. It has two parts: Brand Concepts and the Five Principles for Success.

Tiếng Việt: Rakuten Shugi là hệ thống giá trị và nguyên tắc hành động chung của Rakuten. Gồm 2 phần: 5 giá trị cốt lõi (Brand Concepts) và 5 nguyên tắc để thành công (Five Principles for Success).

Say it in 20 seconds

“To me, Rakuten Shugi is about working with integrity, teamwork, and commitment, while always improving, learning from results, and focusing on customers. I think these values are useful at work and in daily life.”

Ý chính: Làm việc chính trực, hợp tác, có trách nhiệm; luôn cải tiến, học từ kết quả và đặt khách hàng làm trọng tâm.

A. Brand Concepts — 5 core values

1

On a Mission:
Empowerment

Help individuals and businesses grow and succeed.
Trao quyền, tạo giá trị cho người khác.

2

Behave Ethically:
Integrity

Act honestly and do the right thing.
Trung thực, chính trực.

3

Prepare to Succeed:
Professionalism

Prepare well and work professionally.
Chuẩn bị kỹ và làm việc chuyên nghiệp.

4

Complete Commitment:
Get Things Done

Take ownership and finish what matters.
Cam kết đến cùng, hoàn thành mục tiêu.

5

Solidarity:
Succeed as a Team

Collaborate and help one another succeed.
Đoàn kết, hỗ trợ nhau cùng thành công.

B. Five Principles for Success — remember these five

1

Always Improve, Always Advance

Keep improving and challenge yourself; don't stop at “good enough.”

Luôn cải tiến, không ngừng tiến bộ.

Example: After each task, find one way to make the next task better. Sau mỗi task, rút ra một điều để cải thiện lần sau.

2

Passionately Professional

Take responsibility, prepare carefully, and keep improving your skills.

Làm việc với tinh thần chuyên nghiệp, trách nhiệm và chủ động học hỏi.

Example: Understand the business requirement, test your code carefully, and deliver reliable work.

3

Hypothesize → Practice → Validate → Shikumika

Make a hypothesis, try it, check the result, then turn what works into a repeatable process. Shikumika roughly means systemizing.

Đặt giả thuyết → Thực hiện → Kiểm chứng → Chuẩn hóa thành quy trình có thể lặp lại.

Example: Think an API is slow because of a database query → optimize it → measure latency → document the fix as a reusable practice.

4

Maximize Customer Satisfaction

Understand what customers need and continuously improve their experience.

Luôn quan tâm nhu cầu khách hàng và cải thiện trải nghiệm của họ.

Example: Make a credit-card-related process accurate, clear, and easy for users to understand.

5

Speed!! Speed!! Speed!!

Act quickly, make timely decisions, and avoid unnecessary delay, without ignoring quality or safety.

Hành động nhanh, ra quyết định kịp thời; nhanh nhưng vẫn phải đảm bảo chất lượng.

Example: Raise a blocker early, share progress, and deliver small verified improvements instead of waiting too long.

C. Quick Q&A — if someone asks you

Q: What is Rakuten Shugi?

“It is Rakuten's basic philosophy. It combines shared values with five practical principles that help us work better and keep improving.”

Là triết lý nền tảng, kết hợp giá trị cốt lõi và các nguyên tắc hành động để liên tục phát triển.

Q: Which principle do you like most?

“I like ‘Always Improve, Always Advance’ because I believe even small improvements every day can create a big difference over time.”

Em thích nguyên tắc luôn cải tiến vì những bước tiến nhỏ hằng ngày sẽ mang lại sự thay đổi lớn.

Q: How can you use it in daily life?

“I can set a small goal, take action, review the result, and improve. I can also be more responsible and supportive toward my family and friends.”

Đặt mục tiêu nhỏ, hành động, nhìn lại kết quả và cải thiện; có trách nhiệm và hỗ trợ người thân.

Q: What does Shikumika mean?

“For me, it means turning a successful solution into a clear and repeatable process, so the team can use it again.”

Biến giải pháp hiệu quả thành quy trình chuẩn để cả nhóm có thể tái sử dụng.

Q: What have you learned in your first month?

“I've learned about Rakuten's culture, the credit card business, and the RADECO project. I've also learned a lot from my colleagues and their professional way of working.”

Đã học văn hóa công ty, nghiệp vụ thẻ tín dụng, dự án RADECO và cách làm việc chuyên nghiệp từ đồng nghiệp.

Q: How will you apply these principles at work?

“I'll keep learning, ask questions when needed, take ownership of tasks, and work closely with the team to deliver good results.”

Tiếp tục học hỏi, chủ động trao đổi, chịu trách nhiệm với task và phối hợp để tạo ra kết quả tốt.
